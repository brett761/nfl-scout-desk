Who’s better, and by how much.

We rate every NFL team against a league-average club. Then we turn that into a predicted gap for each game and compare it to the sportsbook. If we disagree by about a field goal, we look at a bet. If not, we skip. No favorite teams.

How to read this

  1. Teams — plus is better than average. Minus is worse. Tap a club to see why.
  2. Games — tap a matchup. Open is the first street we logged. Current is today’s sportsbook. B$ Line is the gap we expect.
  3. Copper means we disagree by about a field goal, or we landed on opposite sides of 3 or 7. That is a look, not an automatic bet.
  4. Skip is normal. Most games we do not bet.

      How the number is built · not the recipe

      Methodology

      The B Line is our number for a game: the gap we expect between the two teams, from the home side. It is an opinion we freeze before the kick. It is not a ticket, a price at a book, or a promise.

      The record

      Bet History shows that number only after the game is final. The line on the page is the line we published. If the model changes later, those rows stay. We do not go back and recalculate them. A flagged row was not a clean pre-kick publication, and it stays off the record.

      What the desk looks at

      A lot of things are judged together. None of them is the model by itself.

      • How the teams played in prior seasons.
      • How they have played recently.
      • Offense and defense, not just the final score.
      • Players, and whether the people who matter are available.
      • Injuries and the rest of the personnel picture.
      • The matchup itself.
      • Situational spots, including travel, rest, and short weeks.
      • The market: where the number opened, where it went, and that a price exists.
      • Historical trends that have shown up often enough to respect.
      • Quantitative indicators.
      • Qualitative reads that a box score will not hold.

      Last season, then this one

      Last season is a starting point, not a life sentence. As the new season piles up real games, that older body of work carries less of the conversation. The taper is part of the judgment. The schedule of it is not published here.

      One number

      Those inputs are evaluated together. The output is one number, the B Line. We compare it with the market. A disagreement is a look, not an automatic bet.

      What this page will not do

      This is the public account of the work. It is not the combination. There are no weights, formulas, or scaling rules on this page, and no list of which source is multiplied by what. The next unpublished game stays on the desk.

      Admin only · not the production model

      Testing

      Rerun frozen inputs from games that are already final. The production B Line in the table is the published one. An experimental number lives only in this test.
