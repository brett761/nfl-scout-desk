Who’s better, and by how much.

We rate every NFL team against a league-average club. Then we turn that into a predicted gap for each game and compare it to the sportsbook. If we disagree by about a field goal, we look at a bet. If not, we skip. No favorite teams.

How to read this

  1. Teams — plus is better than average. Minus is worse. Tap a club to see why.
  2. Games — tap a matchup. Our line is the gap we expect. Street is the sportsbook.
  3. Copper means we disagree by about a field goal, or we landed on opposite sides of 3 or 7. That is a look, not an automatic bet.
  4. Skip is normal. Most games we do not bet.

    Words a friend might not know
    Line
    The point handicap. Chiefs −3 means Kansas City must win by more than 3.
    Our line
    The gap we expect, from the home team’s view.
    Street
    What the sportsbook is offering right now.
    Edge
    How far those two numbers are apart.
    Skip
    We looked and did not bet. That counts as a decision.
    Unit
    $50. The week is 20 units ($1,000). 1u look, 2u standard, 3u full, 4u max.
    Straight
    One game, sized in units. This is what we grade.
    Parlay
    Several games at once, 1 unit. For fun, not the score.

    Why a half-point matters

    3 and 7

    NFL games land on field goals (3) and touchdowns (7) a lot. If we sit on one side of that number and the sportsbook sits on the other, the half-point is the whole bet.
