# 2026 AFC Rookie Week 1 Starter Research

**As of:** Thursday, August 20, 2026 (mid-to-late August camp / preseason Week 2)  
**Scope:** 16 AFC clubs only. A "starter" is a player listed **first-team / no OR** on a team unofficial depth chart, or independently confirmed by coaching comments as the Week 1 starter. Depth, rotation, special-teams-only, "might play," and open camp battles = **not** a starter.  
**Draft board sources:** [NFL.com 2026 Round 1 tracker](https://www.nfl.com/draft/tracker/2026/rounds/1) and [Wikipedia: 2026 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NFL_draft) (picks 1–257).  
**Camp sources:** team official unofficial depth charts and August 2026 training-camp / preseason reports cited per player.

**Bar applied strictly.** Several first-rounders (Bailey, Mendoza, Delane, Woods, Mesidor, Lomu, Iheanachor, Faulk, Sadiq, Cooper) will play snaps but are **not** locked Week 1 starters as of Aug 20.
