**As of:** Thursday, August 20, 2026 (mid-to-late August camp / preseason Week 2 window)  
**Scope:** 16 NFC clubs only. Week 1 of the 2026 regular season is Sep 10+.  
**Rule:** starter = expected to start the first regular-season snap at that position. Depth, rotation, special-teams-only, “might play,” and open camp battles are **not** starters (`starter=false`).  
**Sources:** live pages fetched Aug 20, 2026 (NFL.com / Wikipedia draft tracker, team official sites, ESPN, CBS, SI, beat reports). No invented names.

**Headline:** Only **6 rookies** on **5 clubs** clear the Week 1 starter bar as of today. The other 11 NFC clubs have **zero** confirmed Week 1 starter rookies.

## Method / caveats

- Draft names and pick numbers come from the [NFL.com 2026 tracker](https://www.nfl.com/draft/tracker/2026/rounds/1), [Wikipedia 2026 NFL draft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_NFL_draft), and [CBS Sports team-by-team](https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/draft/news/nfl-draft-picks-by-team-2026-full-list/).
- Starter calls use mid-to-late August 2026 unofficial depth charts plus beat/ESPN/CBS/SI camp reports dated Aug 11–20.
- Unofficial depth charts are communications documents, not gospel. This file **does not** promote a rookie to starter just because analysts like him (Miller, Cisse, Price, Trotter, Styles, Love). Open “OR” lines, committees, and “hopeful for Week 1” injuries stay `starter=false`.
- Preseason Week 3 (Aug 28) and the Aug 30 cut to 53 can still move Trotter, Miller, Cisse, Price, Styles, and Lemon. Re-check those six before kickoff.
