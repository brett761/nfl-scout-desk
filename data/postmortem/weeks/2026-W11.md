## F. Emerging Patterns

- **Backup-QB games are where the model loses to the market.** With a QB1 OUT/IR and |gap|≥3, the model is 0/6 ATS: W9 MIN, W10 NYG and BAL (too bullish on the backup), W11 PHI, NYG and BAL (too bearish). The market prices these better in both directions, which reads as rating noise around QB changes rather than a fixed cap that's too big or too small.
- **The rating keeps winning out of sample.** Three straight weeks where the live rating beat every shrink. ARI@KC and NYJ@LAC were rating-driven disagreements that hit.
- **Stacked injury terms over-adjust.** The two biggest W11 terms both combined an IR QB term with a defensive cluster and both hurt. Pool big terms are 18/42 (43%).
- **Home teams swung hard back:** 11–1 after 3–10 in W10. HFA evidence is back to noise (+0.40 at n=139).
- **Late Sunday injury pull after the lock, fourth straight week.**

## G. Misleading / Redundant Variables

- **Rating × pff_ytd** remain correlated. The pool drop for pff_ytd (9.74) is in-sample; the last three live weeks all preferred keeping it.
- **Injury layer** is near zero or slightly negative on the 147-game pool. Big stacked terms look like the misleading part.
- **QB1 OUT pricing by manual** (Murray 0.3 vs auto 1.5) is a judgment call each week. The written rule (floor at the QB1 baseline) and the manual pulled opposite ways, and the auto would have been closer this time.
