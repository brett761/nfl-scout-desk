## F. Emerging Patterns

- **The rating is doing the work, and shrinking it keeps failing out of sample.** Two straight weeks where the model's big favorites (JAX, KC, LAR, DET in W9) came through. The shrink looks like an in-sample fit to W2–W8 blowout misses.
- **Backup-QB favorites the model likes far more than the market: 0/3 straight-up** (W9 MIN, W10 NYG, BAL). In each one the market priced the QB loss harder than our cap or faded IR term did. That's three games, but they all point the same way.
- **Home teams keep losing** (3–10 this week, −0.57 per game since W2). HFA 2.0 is now the clearest overweight on the pool, though still small.
- **Lock-to-close moves went against us 5 of 7.** Without tickets that's only an information-content read: at lock time the market knew something about SF@DAL, PIT@CIN, LAC@BAL, LAR@ARI and WSH@NYG that we didn't price.
- **Late Sunday injury pull after the freeze, third straight week.**

## G. Misleading / Redundant Variables

- **Rating × pff_ytd** remain correlated. Pool ablation still favors trimming pff_ytd, but two straight live weeks preferred keeping both. Treat single-layer drops as overstated.
- **The IR term decays too fast for a team still on its backup** (Dart 0.53 in W10). The rating hasn't absorbed the loss, and the model looks too high on NYG.
- **Madden auto value for a QB1 OUT** (Murray 0.28) is still misleading by construction, even in a week it happened to help.

## H. Missing Information

- **QB1 floor and an IR hold**: never price a QB1 OUT below the QB1 baseline, and hold the IR term until the rating has absorbed the backup's games.
- **In-game QB injury tag** for the ledger (Maye, Geno Smith this week; Lamar W8) so variance is separated from model error.
- **Snap counts** to grade Q rows properly (box stats undercount OL and some defenders).
- **Turnover-luck adjustment**: GB 4, ATL 2, DAL 2, BAL 2, NO 2 this week.
- **Re-freeze after the late Sunday injury pull** when any priced row flips.
- **Week 10 openers file** is missing (only the ESPN open is available).
