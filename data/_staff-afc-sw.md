- **DEN OC Webb** — partial. Wikipedia Davis Webb: Payton said at the 2026 combine that Webb will call plays, first time Payton has delegated that as HC. Offense style still unnamed.

- **DEN ST Rizzi** — unknown.
- **KC OC Bieniemy** — partial. Reid offense (Bieniemy was Reid’s OC 2018–2022). 2026 play-calling split not named on opened pages.
- **KC DC Spagnuolo** — partial. Wikipedia coaching philosophy: 4–3 base, multiple blitz packages (Jim Johnson influence). Not a 2026-only rewrite.
- **KC ST Toub** — unknown.
- **LAC OC McDaniel** — partial. Wikipedia: Shanahan coaching tree. 2026 Chargers play-calling not named.
- **LAC DC O'Leary** — unknown. First NFL DC title (2026). 2024 LAC safeties under Minter; 2025 was college (WMU).
- **LAC ST Ficken** — unknown.
- **LV OC Janocko** — unknown. First OC title. Search snippets claimed Kubiak will call plays; that NFL.com URL was **not opened**, so play-caller is unknown here.
- **LV DC Leonard** — unknown. First NFL DC title (promoted from DL/RGC).
- **LV ST DeCamillis** — unknown.
